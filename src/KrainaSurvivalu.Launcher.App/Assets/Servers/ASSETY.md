# Grafiki kart serwerów

Te oryginalne grafiki promocyjne wygenerowano wbudowanym narzędziem imagegen. Są ilustracją klimatu serwerów, nie zrzutami konkretnych miejsc w DayZ. Obrazy są osadzone w aplikacji; nie wymagają sieci ani ścieżek z komputera autora. Nie zastosowano późniejszej obróbki obrazu, a kadrowanie wykonuje WPF przy wyświetlaniu.

- Badlands: `badlands-v2.png` — piaszczysta pustynia, zgodnie z korektą użytkownika.
- Deer Isle: `deer-isle-v1.png` — zalesione wybrzeże i wyspy.

## Końcowy prompt Badlands

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide photographic promotional landscape banner for a DayZ community launcher, Badlands server. Primary request: unmistakably SAHARA-STYLE DESERT BADLANDS, vast golden SAND DUNES and windblown sand, dry sandstone rock formations, an isolated sun-bleached abandoned settlement made of low sandy adobe and concrete buildings partly buried by sand, a desert dirt track. Arid North African desert survival atmosphere. Cinematic natural photographic realism, amber sun low over dunes, dusty atmospheric distance, warm sand and dark brown palette. Wide horizontal 16:9 image intended to fill a shallow cropped desktop server-card banner; central horizontal strip should clearly show sand dunes and sandy architecture. No forest, no green vegetation, no autumn trees, no lakes, no European mountain villages, no people, no weapons, no text, no words, no logos, no UI, no watermarks. Original illustrative promotional scenery, not an actual map screenshot.

## Końcowy prompt Deer Isle

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide photographic banner for a dark military olive Windows DayZ community launcher, server card called Deer Isle. Create a cinematic realistic abandoned northeastern coastal island landscape: cold Atlantic inlet, dense conifer forest, weathered coastal houses and an old small harbor, rocky shoreline and distant wooded islands with low mist, muted pine green and blue gray colors, natural dramatic early morning light, grounded survival atmosphere. Photographic detail and believable geography. Wide horizontal 16:9 composition intended for a shallow cropped banner, keep the main landscape in the central horizontal strip with readable contrast. No people, no close-up weapons, no text, no words, no logos, no UI, no watermarks. This is original illustrative promotional scenery, not a literal map screenshot.
